The problem

Your AI only knows what you hand it.

That means the chat and the repo. Most of the decisions behind a research project are made somewhere else, so the agent never sees them.

What your agent sees
  • The chatWhatever you remembered to paste
  • The repoThe code, but not the reasons behind it
Where the decisions are
  • NotebooksWhich run worked, and which didn't
  • PapersThe method you're following
  • Past agent sessionsWhat you settled on last week
  • Shared docsThe plan everyone agreed to
  • SpreadsheetsSample logs, parameters
  • Other reposThe lab's shared tooling
Research memory

One knowledge base for the whole project.

Each source feeds one research graph that keeps updating. That gives Rao a specific understanding of your project, and it gives your agents exactly the information they need.

In your project
CodePython, R, TypeScript, SQL, and shell scripts, including how files depend on each other
NotebooksCells and outputs, chunked by cell
Papers & documentsPDFs, LaTeX, Markdown, and Word files, searchable down to the section
DatasetsSchemas for CSV, Parquet, and Excel files. Raw data stays on your machine.
Agent sessionsDecisions and results from your Claude Code, Codex, and Rao sessions
External sources
Google DocsProtocols, plans, and drafts
Google SheetsSample logs, run parameters, and results tables
GitHubRepositories, issues, and pull requests
NotionPages you choose, synced into the project and searched with everything else
EpisodesEach session is condensed into a record of what was explored, decided, or found.
EntitiesEach record links to the files, cells, variables, and papers it touched.
Daily logEvery day, Rao adds a summary of the project and an updated state of the work.
Always currentChanged files are re-indexed in the background while you work.
