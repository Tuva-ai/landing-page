Part 2 · Proactive agents

The digest

Once a week, the digest agent searches new literature for each open question in your project. It suggests a paper only when the paper clearly matters to your work, which usually means one a week and some weeks none.

Built to spark ideas

Each suggestion explains how the paper connects to your work. That connection is often where a new idea starts: a method you could borrow, a result that challenges an assumption, or a test you could run on data you already have.

You decide what it follows

Choose authors, journals, preprint servers, and fields to follow. Read suggestions in the app, get a desktop notification, or turn on a weekly email.

Where Rao runs

Rao runs where your research does: on your laptop, or on your institution's computing cluster.

On your computer

A desktop app for Mac, plus VS Code and the terminal. Your files and research memory stay on your machine.

On the cluster (HPC)

Runs as a single self-contained image under Slurm, with nothing to compile. Connect from your laptop over SSH, so the memory sits next to the data and jobs it describes.
